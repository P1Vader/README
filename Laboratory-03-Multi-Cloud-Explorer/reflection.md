## Reflection

Among the three cloud platforms I explored, Google Cloud Platform impressed me the most because of its strong focus on Artificial Intelligence and Kubernetes. The integration of TensorFlow, TPUs, and advanced machine learning services showed me how powerful GCP can be for research and innovation. At the same time, I recognized that AWS and Azure also have unique strengths—AWS with its massive global infrastructure and service catalog, and Azure with its seamless integration into Microsoft environments.

Despite their differences, AWS, Azure, and GCP share many similarities. All three provide core services in compute, storage, networking, and identity management. Each platform offers a management console that makes it easier to deploy and monitor resources. They also emphasize scalability, security, and reliability, which are essential for modern organizations moving to the cloud.

One of the most important lessons I learned is that business requirements always drive cloud platform selection. A startup with limited budget may benefit from GCP’s cost‑effective solutions, while a university that already uses Microsoft technologies will naturally find Azure more suitable. Enterprises with global operations often rely on AWS because of its reach and maturity. This showed me that there is no “one best cloud”—the right choice depends on the client’s needs.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me how to analyze scenarios and match them with the right services. I practiced thinking beyond technical features and considered how each platform solves real business problems. This experience improved my ability to make professional recommendations.

Finally, my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has grown significantly. By organizing research files, comparison tables, recommendations, and reflections, I now have a structured portfolio that demonstrates both technical knowledge and analytical skills. This mission helped me become more confident in documenting cloud solutions and presenting them in a professional way.
